What this is: the engine’s L0 datasheet score — generalist, 2000pt, rank-decay roster index. No army rules, no detachments, no meta.

Why it is not exact:

  • The engine never compares rule packages numerically — they’re assessed qualitatively per faction in Key Insights (expert files). The + rules heuristics toggle layers that assessment on top as labeled guesswork (STRATEGY tier), not engine output.
  • 11e core-rules fact: cover worsens the attacker’s hit roll (BS +1), it does not modify saves. This view assumes no cover and average dice.
  • 11e matched play uses standardized 16-footprint layouts, three mission maps (A/B/C) per primary-mission pairing; players and TOs report the new tables play more open / less LoS-blocking than the retired WTC/UKTC packs. Map and board-state factors are not modeled here.
  • Codex-vintage skew: 11e is played on 10e codexes — a fresher book rates higher regardless of design intent.
  • DPP is expected value, not a win-rate model.
