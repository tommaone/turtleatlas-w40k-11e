### Army Rule
- **Towering Example**: when the Army Faction is ADEPTUS TITANICUS the army ignores the Select Detachment Rules step; one ADEPTUS TITANICUS model (no CHARACTER keyword needed) is selected as WARLORD; the army plays with the Take and Hold disposition.
- **Play without detachment support**: everything a titan list does comes from its four datasheets' abilities plus raw profile mass. No CP-generating detachment economy, no enhancement layer, no conditional buff windows — the faction has zero modifier surface for the engine to model beyond datasheet stats.
- **Titanic Support ally route**: any IMPERIUM army can include exactly one ADEPTUS TITANICUS model outside its faction keyword via Titanic Support (research). This is the single-model ally slot through which titans actually appear in most matched-play games — the receiving army keeps its own detachments; the allied titan gains no detachment rules either way.

### Enhancements & Stratagems Worth Taking
- None exist — the faction has no enhancement or stratagem layer of its own (research). Titans fielded via the Titanic Support route use the host army's enhancements/stratagems, which never affect the allied titan unless its own datasheet says so.

Overall army play pattern (interpretation): a pure Adeptus Titanicus army is a walking terrain feature — it wins Take and Hold by simply existing on the objectives it can reach, and loses whenever the mission demands breadth, speed or actions. The realistic competitive use of this faction in 11e is the single allied titan: one Warhound-class or Reaver-class model absorbed into an IMPERIUM list where it functions as an unkillable scoring anchor or long-range fire support, while the host army provides everything the titan cannot do — board width, actions and objective flexibility. Any assessment treating titan-legions as a normal detachment-selecting faction is misreading the pack by design.
