# Chaos Daemons — 11th Edition Expert

Chaos Daemons are warp entities of the four Chaos Gods (Khorne, Tzeentch, Nurgle,
Slaanesh) plus Undivided. Army strengths: Deep Strike across whole faction,
god-specific specialization, 4++ invuln natively, and Shadow of Chaos board control.

**Key characteristics:**
- All units have 4++ invuln (some 5++)
- All units have Deep Strike natively
- No shooting for most — primarily melee + psychic
- Split into 4 mono-god sub-factions + Undivided (Be'lakor)
- Shadow of Chaos is the core army rule: conditional board-wide aura

---

## Army Rules

### The Shadow of Chaos
If your Army Faction is Legiones Daemonica, certain areas are in your Shadow of Chaos:
- **Your deployment zone:** always
- **No Man's Land:** if you control ≥ half objectives in NML (start of phase, until end)
- **Enemy deployment zone:** if you control ≥ half objectives in enemy DZ (start of phase, until end)

### Daemonic Manifestation
While a LEGIONES DAEMONICA unit is within your army's Shadow of Chaos:
- Each time it takes a Battle-shock test: +1 to the test
- If test is passed: one model regains up to D3 lost wounds
- If Battleline unit and test passed: up to D3 destroyed models returned instead

### Daemonic Terror
While an enemy unit is within your army's Shadow of Chaos AND/OR within 6" of a
named Greater Daemon (Bloodthirster, Great Unclean One, Kairos, Keeper, Lord of
Change, Rotigus, Shalaxi Helbane, Skarbrand):
- Each time it takes a Battle-shock test: -1 to that test
- If test is failed: that enemy unit suffers D3 mortal wounds

### Daemonic Pact (Allies)
If every model in your army has the CHAOS KNIGHTS or HERETIC ASTARTES keyword,
can include LEGIONES DAEMONICA units:
- Incursion: up to 250pts
- Strike Force: up to 500pts
- Onslaught: up to 750pts
- Cannot be Warlord, cannot get Enhancements
- Battleline tax: non-Battleline per god ≤ Battleline per god

**Critical:** Allied Daemons do NOT get Shadow of Chaos — gated on "If your Army
Faction is Legiones Daemonica". They keep CORE abilities + individual datasheet
abilities only.

## Detachments

Daemons have 9 detachments in 11e. DP budget: typically 2-3 DP at 2000pts.

### Daemonic Incursion (1DP)
**Rule — Warp Rifts:** When a unit sets up via Deep Strike wholly within Shadow of
Chaos and/or within 6" of a matching-god Greater Daemon, it can come in >6"
instead of >9".
**Stratagems:** Corrupt Realspace (1CP), Warp Surge (1CP), Draught of Terror (1CP),
Denizens of the Warp (1CP), The Realm of Chaos (1CP), Daemonic Invulnerability (1CP)
**Enhancements:** A'rgath (Khorne, +A/S melee), The Everstave (Tzeentch, +S/R),
The Endless Gift (Nurgle, FNP5+), Soulstealer (Slaanesh, regen on kill)

### Shadow Legion (Be'lakor — 1DP)
**Restriction:** Cannot include Daemon Prince, Daemon Prince with Wings, or any
other Epic Heroes except Be'lakor.
**Rule — First Prince of Chaos:** Units gain god-specific keywords and rules:
- Khorne: Murderer's Cowl (Advance/charge/shoot)
- Tzeentch: Penumbral Puppetry (Stealth, -1 hit in melee)
- Nurgle: Gloam Rot (S > T = -1 to wound)
- Slaanesh: Shadow's Caress (can't be snap shot)
- Undivided: Dark Pacts, Deep Strike for HA units
**Allies:** Can include CSM Heretic Astartes units (Legionaries, Chosen, Possessed,
Terminators, etc.) up to 500/1000/1500pts per battle size.
**Stratagems:** Spiteful Demise (1CP), Channelled Wrath (1CP), Death Denied (1CP),
Encroaching Darkness (1CP), Shade Path (2CP), Binding Shadow (1CP)
**Enhancements:** Leaping Shadows (Scouts 9"), Mantle of Gloom (Aura, -1 OC),
Fade to Darkness (SR after fight), Malice Made Manifest (mortals in fight)

### Cavalcade of Chaos (Mounted focus — 1DP)
**Rule — Unholy Avalanche:** MOUNTED units that Fall Back can still shoot/charge.
**Enhancements:** Apocalyptic Steeds (+1" M), Soul-Shattering Charge (pile-in 3")
**Stratagems:** From Beyond the Veil (1CP, ingress), Warp-Riders (1CP, Mobile),
Inescapable Manifestations (1CP, desperate escape on fall back)

### Lords of the Warp (Character focus — 1DP)
**Rule — Loci of Power:** CHARACTER non-MONSTER models have +1 LD and +1 OC.
**Enhancements:** Swollen With Power (+2 W)
**Stratagems:** Carnival of Excess (1CP, Fights First Slaanesh character),
Call to Murder (1CP, +1 A Khorne character), Bilious Blessing (1CP, mortals
Nurgle character), Skirling Magicks (1CP, Lethal Hits Tzeentch character)

### Warptide (Battleline focus — 1DP)
**Rule — Shudderblink:** When BATTLELINE unit Advances, its ranged has [ASSAULT]

**Enhancements:** Bane-Forged Weapons (+1 S), Soul-Hungry Slaughterers (-1 CP overwatch)
**Stratagems:** Daemonic Infestation (1CP, heal 3), Soulseeing (1CP, +6" detection),

## Key Modeling Notes

1. **Daemonic Pact allies do NOT get Shadow of Chaos** — the army rule is gated
   on "If your Army Faction is Legiones Daemonica". Allied daemons in a CK/CSM
   army keep CORE abilities and individual datasheet abilities only. They do not
   benefit from Daemonic Manifestation, Daemonic Terror, or any detachment rule.

2. **Be'lakor IS Legiones Daemonica** — when taken as a CK ally, he keeps his
   datasheet abilities (The Dark Master aura extends Shadow of Chaos 6",
   Shadow Form selection) but the Daemon army rule Shadow of Chaos only covers
   the default areas (DZ, NML with control, enemy DZ with control — all gated
   on "If your Army Faction is Legiones Daemonica", which fails in CK/CSM).

3. **Horrors Split is not recursion** — Split is a "when destroyed" trigger, not
   a "spawn new unit". Models added can exceed starting strength. When no Pink
   Horrors remain, switch to Blue Horrors datasheet. Cannot return Pink Horrors
   to a Blue Horror unit via any method.

4. **Soul Grinder has no native god keyword** — must select Daemonic Allegiance
   on army selection. This determines its additional weapon and keyword.

5. **Daemon Prince has Lone Operative when near Infantry** — "Daemonic Lord":
   while within 3" of friendly Legiones Daemonica Infantry, has Lone Operative.

6. **Greater Daemon auras extend Shadow of Chaos** — each god's Greater Daemon
   (Bloodthirster, Lord of Change, Great Unclean One, Keeper of Secrets) has
   a 6" aura: "While a friendly X Legiones Daemonica unit is within 6" of this
   model, that unit is within your army's Shadow of Chaos." This is critical
   for expanding Shadow of Chaos beyond objective control.

7. **Skarbrand gives +1 A melee** to Khorne units within 6" (Rage Embodied aura).
   This compounds with Bloodthirster's +1 to hit (Daemon Lord of Khorne).

8. **Faction Pack datasheets are authoritative** — all weapon profiles above
   are from the 11e FP PDF, not BSData 10e.

9. **No T1 Deep Strike** — standard Reserves restriction. Affects mobility
   scoring on deployment-heavy missions.

10. **Fortifications (Skull Altar, Feculent Gnarlmaw)** — provide SoC extension
    and Cover. Skull Altar gives SoC within 6". Gnarlmaw gives Stealth to
    Nurgle units within 6". Both provide Cover vs ranged through obscured.

## Gotchas

### Shadow of Chaos area depends on board control
Your DZ is always SoC. NML and enemy DZ require objective control thresholds.
This makes SoC unreliable early game against a spread-out opponent.

### Greater Daemon keyword requirements for Warp Rifts
Daemonic Incursion's "Denizens of the Warp" lets Deep Striking units come in
>6" if wholly within SoC AND/OR within 6" of a matching-god Greater Daemon.
The Greater Daemon must share the Khorne/Tzeentch/Nurgle/Slaanesh keyword
with the arriving unit.

### Be'lakor Supreme Commander
Be'lakor MUST be your Warlord if in the army. Cannot be taken as a non-Warlord
in a Daemons army, but as a CK ally he is not your Warlord anyway (allied
Daemons cannot be Warlord per Daemonic Pact).

### Flux tokens are shared resources
Scintillating Legion's Fates in Flux gives your opponent your Flux tokens.
Spending discipline is critical — don't give the opponent re-rolls carelessly.

### Plague Legion's Melancholic Miasma vs Daemonic Terror
Plague Legion's detachment rule forces an enemy unit to take a Battle-shock
test each Command phase. If they fail, Daemonic Terror triggers D3 mortals
(if they're also within 6" of a named Greater Daemon). The Cankerblight
enhancement explicitly says "no mortal wounds are inflicted by your army's
Daemonic Terror rule" when used — it replaces the Terror mortals with
its own destroy-one-model effect.

### Legion of Excess Seductive Gambit trades Fights First
This is a meaningful trade — Daemonettes and characters have native Fights
First. Opting into Seductive Gambit loses that for re-rolls. Choose per charge.

### Soul Grinder Scuttling Walker
Can move over friendly Monster/Vehicle models and terrain ≤4" height freely.
This is a mobility advantage not reflected in base MOB scoring.

### 🟡 A-Tier

**Khorne: Blood Legion + Warptide** (2+1, Purge)
- Murdercall for reactive positioning. Warptide gives Bloodletters advance+charge.
- Purge disposition. Khorne wants to kill everything.

**Tzeentch: Scintillating Legion + Warptide** (2+1, Priority)
- Flux tokens for massive re-roll flexibility. Warptide gives Horrors advance+charge.
- Priority Assets to protect Lords of Change.

**Mixed: Shadow Legion** (2DP, Purge) + **Cavalcade of Chaos** (1DP, Purge)
- Be'lakor + CSM allies + mounted daemons. Purge disposition.
- Most flexible list-building. Can take any god's units plus CSM.

**Slaanesh: Legion of Excess + Cavalcade of Chaos** (2+1, Priority)
- Seductive Gambit re-rolls + Mounted fallback for Seekers/Chariots.
- Priority Assets. Speed-focused.

### 🟠 B-Tier

**Undivided: Daemonic Incursion** (3DP, Disrupt)
- Universal Deep Strike buff. Any god works. But 3DP with no secondary is expensive.
- Disruption disposition is not ideal.

### C-Tier

**Anything with Lords of the Warp** as your only 1DP — its rule (+1 Ld +1 OC on characters) is the weakest in the book.

## Key Strategic Notes

- **Warptide (1DP) is the best value pick** — it gives advance+charge to ALL BATTLELINE units. Every god has battleline. It fits every army.
- **Shadow Legion requires BE'LAKOR as Warlord** and restricts other Daemon Princes/EPIC HEROES. The CSM ally allowance is up to 1000pts at Strike Force.
- **Daemons have baseline 4+ invuln** (or 5+ for some units) — this is baked into their datasheets, not detachment rules. Don't confuse the two.
- **Nurgle Plague Legion's Melancholic Miasma is NOT a defensive buff** — it's a board control + battleshock tool. Don't model it as Stealth.
- **Flux tokens (Scintillating) can be used by your opponent** — there's risk/reward in hoarding them vs spending them.
- **Blood Legion has TWO detachment rules** — Murdercall (reactive surge) AND Blood Tainted (objective control). Most players forget Blood Tainted.

### Detachment Assessments

#### Cavalcade Of Chaos (1DP → DISRUPTION)
- **Mechanics**: Unholy Avalanche — MOUNTED daemon units that Fell Back this turn remain eligible to shoot and to declare a charge.
- **Rating**: Situational for Disruption
- **Synergies**: Slaanesh-mounted packages (Seekers, Hellflayers) hit-and-run repeatedly.
- **Limits**: MOUNTED filter only; fall-back eligibility has no numeric modifier expression (advance_and_charge covers Advance, not Fall Back, per research).
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/chaos-daemons/

#### Lords Of The Warp (1DP → TAKE AND HOLD)
- **Mechanics**: Loci of Power — friendly LEGIONES DAEMONICA CHARACTER models (excluding MONSTERS) get +1 Leadership and +1 Objective Control.
- **Rating**: Weak for Take and Hold
- **Synergies**: Herald-type characters babysitting home objectives.
- **Limits**: Character-only, non-MONSTER scope; engine must restrict oc_boost to characters (research notes old config left it inert); +1 OC rarely moves primary scoring on its own.
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/chaos-daemons/

#### Warptide (1DP → RECONNAISSANCE)
- **Mechanics**: Shudderblink — when a BATTLELINE daemon unit Advances, its ranged attacks gain ASSAULT that turn and the Advance does not stop it declaring a charge.
- **Rating**: Moderate for Reconnaissance
- **Synergies**: Any battleline core — Bloodletters, Daemonettes, Plaguebearers, Pink Horrors — becomes a fast scoring threat.
- **Limits**: BATTLELINE units only (engine must restrict advance_and_charge accordingly per research); ASSAULT-on-Advance shooting effect not numerically modeled.
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/chaos-daemons/

#### Blood Legion (2DP → PURGE THE FOE)
- **Mechanics**: Two rules. Murdercall — when an enemy unit ends a move within 8" of friendly KHORNE daemons in the opponent's Movement phase, one such KHORNE unit may surge-move up to D6". Blood Tainted — objectives stay under your control after KHORNE units destroy enemies on them until the opponent's control exceeds yours.
- **Rating**: Situational for Purge the Foe (Khorne-heavy lists)
- **Synergies**: Bloodletters / Bloodcrushers / Skulltaker forward lines; Blood Tainted makes kills on objectives stick for T&H crossover value.
- **Limits**: Both halves are KHORNE-scoped; Murdercall is reactive once-per-trigger, not a speed stat (research stale note).
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/chaos-daemons/

#### Legion Of Excess (2DP → PRIORITY ASSETS)
- **Mechanics**: Beguiling Aura — SLAANESH units may charge after Falling Back. Seductive Gambit — when a SLAANESH unit ends a charge, declare the Gambit: lose Fights First until end of turn, gain full hit re-rolls and wound re-rolls of 1.
- **Rating**: Situational for Priority Assets
- **Synergies**: Shalaxi Helbane / Keeper Of Secrets / Fiends hit-and-run cycles; Gambit turns a charging Daemonette block into a full-reroll blender.
- **Limits**: SLAANESH-only scope; Gambit is voluntary per-charge and trades away Fights First (research flags old always-on rerolls modeling as wrong).
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/chaos-daemons/

#### Plague Legion (2DP → TAKE AND HOLD)
- **Mechanics**: Melancholic Miasma — enemies within 9" of friendly NURGLE daemons count as inside your Shadow of Chaos; each Command phase one enemy unit in your Shadow of Chaos must take a battle-shock test.
- **Rating**: Situational for Take and Hold
- **Synergies**: Plaguebearers / Nurglings / Great Unclean One walls spreading the aura; interacts with Daemonic Terror per research notes (unmodeled).
- **Limits**: Pure morale/board-control mechanics — no DPP/SURV/MOB/OBJ expression; NURGLE-scoped.
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/chaos-daemons/

#### Scintillating Legion (2DP → PRIORITY ASSETS)
- **Mechanics**: Fates in Flux — start with three Flux tokens; spend one to re-roll any Advance/Hit/Wound/Damage/save/Hazardous roll for a TZEENTCH unit; each spend gives the opponent a token they can spend too; you regain one per Command phase while they hold any.
- **Rating**: Situational for Priority Assets
- **Synergies**: Lord Of Change / Kairos Fateweaver / Pink Horrors shooting phases where tokens convert failed key rolls.
- **Limits**: Finite shared resource economy — research explicitly states the old reroll_hits:'all' config materially overestimated it; tokens also feed Tzeentch stratagems (CP-dependent).
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/chaos-daemons/

#### Shadow Legion (2DP → PURGE THE FOE)
- **Mechanics**: Be'lakor-themed muster restrictions plus five keyword-scoped abilities: KHORNE units shoot/charge after Advancing; TZEENTCH units get Stealth and -1 vs melee hits; NURGLE units subtract 1 from incoming wound rolls when attacker S > T; SLAANESH units can't be snap-shot; UNDIVIDED units get Dark Pacts and HERETIC ASTARTES models Deep Strike.
- **Rating**: Moderate for Purge the Foe
- **Synergies**: Mixed-god lists built under Be'Lakor — Bloodletters with advance-and-shoot/charge are the standout purge piece.
- **Limits**: Every ability is keyword-scoped, none army-wide (research keeps all out of numeric modeling); muster restrictions constrain list building.
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/chaos-daemons/

#### Daemonic Incursion (3DP → DISRUPTION)
- **Mechanics**: Warp Rifts — LEGIONES DAEMONICA units set up via Deep Strike deploy more than 6" from enemies (instead of more than 8") when wholly within your Shadow of Chaos and/or within 6" of a matching-god Greater Daemon-type unit.
- **Rating**: Situational for Disruption
- **Synergies**: Bloodletters / Daemonettes deep striking beside Bloodthirster / Keeper Of Secrets / Skarbrand-type anchors; Corrupt Realspace extends Shadow of Chaos around objectives (CP-dependent).
- **Limits**: Conditional placement improvement, not reach or damage; at 3DP it competes against detachments with broader effects; old config movement_bonus modeling overstated it (research stale note).
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/chaos-daemons/

### Enhancements & Stratagems Worth Taking
- *(Interpretation, from research file mentions only)* Corrupt Realspace (extends Shadow of Chaos around controlled objectives) is the named CP tool that makes Daemonic Incursion functional; Warp Surge grants charge-after-Advance. Both are CP-dependent — budget accordingly rather than treating them as always available.

### Overall Army Play Pattern
*(interpretation)* Chaos Daemons play as a set of god-specific sub-armies wearing one codex: nearly every detachment rule is keyword-filtered, so the army wants to commit to Khorne melee pressure (Blood Legion), Nurgle morale/board control (Plague Legion), Slaanesh mobility tricks (Legion Of Excess) or Tzeentch reroll economy (Scintillating Legion) rather than split gods. The best general-purpose picks are the ones that ignore god lines — Warptide's battleline advance-and-charge and Shadow Legion's Be'lakor bundle — which is why the faction's fit concentrates in Purge the Foe and Reconnaissance while its Take and Hold options are morale tools whose value never shows up in raw damage math.
