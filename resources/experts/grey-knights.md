## Generic Red Flags (all GK units)

- **Missing Gate of Infinity**: EVERY Grey Knight unit should have Gate of Infinity rule. If a GK-specific unit lacks it, that's a bug.
- **Missing Psychic keyword**: GK weapons should have [Psychic] keyword on Nemesis force weapons and psychic powers.
- **Wrong invulnerable save**: Terminator armour = 4+ invuln. Power armour characters = 4+ invuln. Power armour infantry = no invuln. Dreadknight = 4+ invuln.
- **Wrong BS/WS for units**: Paladins WS2+/BS2+, Terminators WS3+/BS3+, Characters WS2+/BS2+, Power Armour squads WS3+/BS3+
- **Missing Faction: Adeptus Astartes**: All GK units must have this keyword.
- **False OC values**: Battleline = 2, most infantry = 1, vehicles = 0-5. If a Strike Squad has OC1, that's wrong.
- **Techmarine missing abilities**: Must have Techmarine and Blessing of the Omnissiah.
- **Rhino without Truesilver Aegis**: GK Rhino gets Truesilver Aegis (Psychic), not Self-repair.

## Known Tricky Areas

- **Purifying Flame distribution**: EVERY Purifier model has it, including those with heavy weapons. Purgation Squad does NOT have it.
- **NFW vs CCW**: Purgators with heavy weapons swap Nemesis force weapon for Close combat weapon. All other GK keep NFW even with heavy weapons.
- **Rhino ability differs**: GK Rhino has Truesilver Aegis (Psychic). Regular SM Rhino has Self-repair. If our GK Rhino has Self-repair, it's from the wrong datasheet.
- **Brotherhood Champion NFW**: has Precision keyword on the Nemesis force weapon — unique to Champion.
- **Paladin vs Terminator**: Paladins are WS2+/BS2+ (elite), Terminators are WS3+/BS3+ (standard). Both have W3 T5 InSv4+.
- **Strike Squad Scouts**: Only Strike Squad has Scouts. Interceptors/Purgators/Purifiers do NOT have Scouts.
- **Grand Master Voldus Stealth**: Voldus has Stealth rule, other terminator characters don't.
- **Aircraft M/OC**: Stormhawk and Stormtalon have M"-" and OC"-" (not 0). Stormraven has M14" and OC0.
- **Dreadknight Type**: Nemesis Dreadknight has both Monster and Vehicle keywords. GMNDK also has Character.
