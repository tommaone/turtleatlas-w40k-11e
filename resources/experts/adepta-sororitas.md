### Army Rule
- **Acts of Faith / Miracle dice**: Units perform Acts of Faith per phase; Miracle dice generated by the army can be substituted for dice rolls. The pool size and substitution rate — not flat stat buffs — drive faction output.
- **Play pattern**: *(interpretation)* Sisters lists want many small faith-generating units in play so the Miracle dice pool stays deep; detachment choice decides whether you widen the substitution band (Army Of Faith), lean on raw stat detachments instead, or both.

### Detachment Assessments
<!-- one block per detachment, ordered by DP -->

#### Chorus Of Condemnation (1DP → RECONNAISSANCE)
- **Mechanics**: Friendly Sororitas Infantry Fly units may mark one visible enemy within 12" during Shooting as condemned; condemned enemies have +3" detection range for targeting purposes.
- **Rating**: Situational for Reconnaissance
- **Synergies**: Seraphim Squad and Zephyrim Squad as forward markers supporting the rest of the army's shooting.
- **Limits**: Requires an INFANTRY FLY unit within 12" with line of sight; one target per phase; detection-range mechanic has no modifier equivalent.
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/adepta-sororitas/

#### Sacred Champions (1DP → TAKE AND HOLD)
- **Mechanics**: All friendly Celestian Sacresants units' attacks get +1 Ballistic Skill and +1 Weapon Skill.
- **Rating**: Situational for Take and Hold
- **Synergies**: Celestian Sacresants as the sole beneficiary; pairs with Character leaders that buff the same block.
- **Limits**: Scoped entirely to one elite battleline datasheet — every other unit receives nothing from the detachment rule; melee half of the buff is not captured by shooting-oriented modifiers.
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/adepta-sororitas/

#### Sanctified Orators (1DP → DISRUPTION)
- **Mechanics**: Enhancements from this detachment are free (do not count against the enhancement limit) and all friendly Sororitas CHARACTER units get +1 Leadership.
- **Rating**: Weak for Disruption
- **Synergies**: Enhancement-dense Character builds (multiple Canoness-style leaders).
- **Limits**: No direct combat-stat impact; value is list-building points economy only; Leadership is rarely a decisive stat in 11e.
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/adepta-sororitas/

#### Army Of Faith (2DP → TAKE AND HOLD)
- **Mechanics**: Each Sororitas unit may perform two Acts of Faith per phase instead of one — doubling the Miracle dice substitution rate across hits, wounds, saves, charges, advances and morale.
- **Rating**: Situational for Take and Hold
- **Synergies**: Broad infantry spreads (Battle Sisters Squad, Sisters Novitiate Squad) generating and spending Miracle dice every phase.
- **Limits**: No deterministic stat change at all — value depends entirely on live Miracle dice pool state; empty-pool turns get nothing.
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/adepta-sororitas/

#### Bringers Of Flame (2DP → PRIORITY ASSETS)
- **Mechanics**: All ranged weapons on Sororitas models gain the Assault ability (shoot after Advancing); ranged attacks against targets within 6" gain +1 Strength.
- **Rating**: Moderate for Priority Assets
- **Synergies**: Dominion Squad (flamer/melta special weapons), Immolator twin-flamer hulls, Retributor Squad close-range melta shots.
- **Limits**: +1 Strength applies only within 6" — range-gated and target-stat dependent (does nothing once S already equals/greater than needed bands shift); Assault has no vocabulary key.
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/adepta-sororitas/

#### Champions Of Faith (2DP → DISRUPTION)
- **Mechanics**: Each Command phase mark up to 3 units as Righteous until your next Command phase: +1" Move and +1 Leadership; Battle Sisters Squad, Celestian Sacresant and Paragon Warsuits models in them additionally get +1 WS and +1 BS.
- **Rating**: Situational for Disruption
- **Synergies**: Rotating buff across a Battle Sisters Squad / Sacresants / Paragon Warsuits trio.
- **Limits**: Minority-of-army coverage (3 units max); the WS/BS half applies to exactly three datasheets; rotating window means never army-wide.
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/adepta-sororitas/

#### Penitent Host (2DP → PURGE THE FOE)
- **Mechanics**: Each battle round pick one of three Vows (each usable once per battle): PENITENT models +3" Move; PENITENT units that charged this turn +1 Attacks and +1 Strength in melee; destroyed-by-melee PENITENT models fight before removal on a 2+.
- **Rating**: Situational for Purge the Foe
- **Synergies**: Repentia Squad and Arco-Flagellants melee waves with the charge vow timed into the decisive turn.
- **Limits**: All effects restricted to PENITENT keyword models; each vow fires once per battle — depleting resource; fight-on-death vow is a 2+ probability event.
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/adepta-sororitas/

#### Hallowed Martyrs (3DP → PRIORITY ASSETS)
- **Mechanics**: Every attack made by a model whose unit is below Starting Strength gets +1 to hit; once Below Half-strength it gains +1 to wound as well.
- **Rating**: Moderate for Priority Assets
- **Synergies:** Durable grind bodies intended to take casualties — Celestian Insidiants and Sanctifiers (both engine-ranked across dispositions) are strongest mid-game when already damaged.
- **Limits**: Full-strength units get nothing — rule rewards being damaged, which conflicts with keeping units healthy; value scales over game state and cannot be modeled always-on; 3DP cost.
- **_source**: https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/adepta-sororitas/

### Overall Play Pattern
*(interpretation)* Adepta Sororitas is a mid-range aggressive army whose real resource is the Miracle dice pool rather than statlines. The detachment roster splits cleanly: economy wideners (Army Of Faith), positioning/scouting enablers (Chorus Of Condemnation, jump-pack units), and conditional stat detachments that reward either short-range aggression (Bringers Of Flame) or deliberate attrition (Hallowed Martyrs). Because most detachment rules are scoped to keyword subsets or damage states, Sisters players commit to one plan per list — the faction punishes generic all-comers builds that spread buffs thin.
