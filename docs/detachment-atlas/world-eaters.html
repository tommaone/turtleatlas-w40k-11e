Rule source: https://www.40k.app/factions/world-eaters/detachments/brazen-engines. 11e World Eaters Faction Pack Review (2026-06-10, https://www.tabletopbattles.com/40k-11th-edition-faction-pack-review-world-eaters): "There's great stuff in here for buffing your Daemon vehicles, and while the Battle-shock effect is only okay, it'll come up and do useful things", the rule is "ok, and useful for stopping things like Counter-offensive, or offensive Stratagems"; the review also rates Apoplectic Clarity "pretty great" and Trail of Destruction "Great for getting about the table with your gigantic bases"; "This one has Purge the Foe as its Disposition". Limitations
Everything keys to the DAEMON VEHICLE keyword — only Maulerfiends/Forgefiends and Daemon-upgraded vehicles benefit; non-vehicle units get nothing from the Rampaging Terrors rule, Battle-shock effect requires the enemy unit to be engaged with a Daemon Vehicle and fires only at the start of the Fight phase (one enemy unit per unit per phase), onslaught tag — cannot be combined with another onslaught detachment (e.g. Goretrack Onslaught)
