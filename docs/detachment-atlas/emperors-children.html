EMPEROR'S CHILDREN units gain cumulative bonuses per Pact points gained this battle: 1+ re-roll Hit rolls of 1; 3+ re-roll Wound rolls of 1; 5+ melee weapons get LETHAL HITS and SUSTAINED HITS 1; 7+ Critical Hits on unmodified Hit rolls of 5+. (_paraphrase: True, _lang: en)

Strong
Strength notes
11e Goonhammer/Tabletop Battles faction pack review: 'Coterie at 3 DP is tough but given it's already got Purge the Foe, my bet is that most EC lists will just stick with it' (analyst expects it as the default EC pick): https://www.tabletopbattles.com/40k-11th-edition-faction-pack-review-emperors-children/.
